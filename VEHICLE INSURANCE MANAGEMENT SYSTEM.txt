Imagine a vehicle insurance company. People come in and want to
insure their cars. Right now, everything is done on paper. The
agent writes down the customer name, car details, policy number,
payment date, claim information, and so on. This is very slow,
error-prone, and hard to manage.

The company said: "Let us build a software system so that all
insurance records are stored digitally. Any authorized employee
can open the system, search a customer, generate a policy, track
a claim, and print a bill in seconds."

That software is called the Vehicle Insurance Management System.

So in simple words:
This is a web application for a vehicle insurance company. It
handles:
- Registering customers and their vehicles
- Creating insurance policies for customers
- Generating estimates (quotes) before buying a policy
- Processing payments and generating bills
- Handling accident claims
- Tracking company expenses
- Generating reports for management

					----------------------------------------------------------------
ROLES

In this system, there are 2 main roles:


ROLE 1: ADMIN / EMPLOYEE (The Insurance Company Staff)
-------------------------------------------------------
This is the most powerful user. The admin manages everything
happening inside the company.

What the Admin can do:
- Login to the admin panel with admin username and password
- Add, edit, delete customer records
- Add and manage vehicle information
- Create and manage insurance policies
- Generate and manage billing documents
- Handle and approve insurance claims
- Record company expenses
- View and generate all reports
- Manage policy renewals and lapsed policies

Admin Panel Contains:
- Dashboard (overview of active policies, pending claims,
  revenue, upcoming renewals)
- Customer Management
- Vehicle Management
- Estimate / Quote Management
- Insurance Policy Management
- Billing Management
- Claims Management
- Company Expenses
- Reports Section


ROLE 2: CUSTOMER (The Vehicle Owner Who Buys Insurance)
---------------------------------------------------------
The customer registers on the online portal and can manage
their own insurance from their panel.

What the Customer can do:
- Register on the portal with their information
- Login with their unique username and password
- Change their password
- Purchase a vehicle insurance policy online by filling
  an application form
- Make payment online for the policy
- Download and save a copy of their policy document
- Initiate / file a claim when an accident happens by
  filling the claim form
- View their policy status and billing history

Customer Panel Contains:
- My Profile
- Buy New Policy (application form + payment)
- My Policies (active and expired)
- File a Claim (claim form)
- My Claims (status of filed claims)
- Payment History / Bills

PROJECT FLOW
STEP 1: Customer registers on the portal
A vehicle owner visits the insurance company website. They fill
in a registration form with their personal details and create a
username and password. Their account is created.

STEP 2: Customer requests an estimate / quote
Before buying a policy, the customer wants to know how much it
will cost. They provide their vehicle details and the system
generates an estimate. This estimate shows the vehicle rate,
policy type, warranty, and premium amount.

STEP 3: Customer fills the insurance application form
The customer decides to buy a policy. They fill in the online
application form which captures all required information:
personal details, vehicle details, policy type, policy duration.

STEP 4: Customer makes payment online
The customer pays the premium amount online. On successful
payment, the policy is activated in the system.

STEP 5: Policy document is generated
The system generates a policy document with a unique policy
number, policy start and end date, and all vehicle and customer
details. The customer can download and save this.

STEP 6: Admin manages the policy records
The admin (employee) logs into the admin panel and can see all
active policies, their payment schedules, and upcoming renewals.

STEP 7: Customer files a claim (if accident happens)
If the customer's vehicle is in an accident, they log in and
fill the claim form with: place of accident, date of accident,
description of damage, and the claimable amount.

STEP 8: Admin processes the claim
The admin reviews the claim, verifies the policy details, checks
if it is within the policy period, and approves or rejects the
claim. The claimable amount is processed.

STEP 9: Company records its expenses
The admin records all internal company expenses: type of expense,
amount, and date. This helps in financial tracking.

STEP 10: Reports are generated for management
At the end of the month, the admin generates reports:
- How many policies were sold
- Which vehicle type had the most policies
- How many claims were filed and approved
- Which policies are due for renewal
- Which policies have lapsed (expired without renewal)

DATABASE MODELS


MODEL 1: User
--------------
Stores all users of the system (admin and customers).

Fields:
- id
- name
- email
- username
- password (hashed)
- role (admin or customer)
- phone
- created_at
- updated_at


MODEL 2: Customer
------------------
Stores detailed customer profile information.

Fields:
- id
- user_id (links to User)
- customer_name
- address
- phone_number
- address_proof (document reference)
- created_at
- updated_at


MODEL 3: Vehicle
-----------------
Stores all vehicle information belonging to a customer.

Fields:
- id
- customer_id (links to Customer)
- vehicle_name
- owner_name
- vehicle_model
- vehicle_version
- vehicle_rate (value of the vehicle)
- body_number
- engine_number
- vehicle_number (registration number)
- created_at
- updated_at


MODEL 4: Estimate
------------------
Stores the insurance estimate / quote generated for a customer
before they buy a policy.

Fields:
- id
- customer_id (links to Customer)
- estimate_number (unique)
- customer_name
- phone_number
- vehicle_name
- vehicle_model
- vehicle_rate
- vehicle_warranty
- policy_type (comprehensive / third-party / etc.)
- estimated_premium_amount
- created_at
- updated_at


MODEL 5: InsurancePolicy
--------------------------
Stores the actual policy record after the customer purchases it.

Fields:
- id
- customer_id (links to Customer)
- vehicle_id (links to Vehicle)
- policy_number (unique)
- policy_date (start date)
- policy_duration (in months or years)
- policy_end_date
- policy_type
- vehicle_rate
- vehicle_warranty
- body_number
- engine_number
- vehicle_number
- vehicle_name
- vehicle_model
- vehicle_version
- address_proof
- status (active / expired / lapsed / cancelled)
- created_at
- updated_at


MODEL 6: Billing
-----------------
Stores payment and billing records for each policy.

Fields:
- id
- customer_id (links to Customer)
- policy_id (links to InsurancePolicy)
- bill_number (unique)
- vehicle_name
- vehicle_model
- vehicle_rate
- body_number
- engine_number
- amount
- payment_date
- address_proof
- created_at
- updated_at


MODEL 7: Claim
---------------
Stores insurance claim details filed by customers.

Fields:
- id
- claim_number (unique)
- policy_id (links to InsurancePolicy)
- policy_number
- policy_start_date
- policy_end_date
- customer_name
- place_of_accident
- date_of_accident
- insured_amount
- claimable_amount
- status (pending / approved / rejected)
- processed_by (links to admin User)
- created_at
- updated_at


MODEL 8: CompanyExpense
------------------------
Stores company's internal expenses for financial tracking.

Fields:
- id
- date_of_expense
- type_of_expense
- amount_of_expense
- recorded_by (links to admin User)
- created_at
- updated_at


MODEL 9: Report (Generated dynamically from existing data)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Reports are generated from the data above. No separate table
is strictly needed, but if snapshots are required:

Fields:
- id
- report_type (monthly_sales / vehicle_wise / claims /
               renewals_due / lapsed_policies)
- generated_by
- generated_at
- data (JSON or file reference)

CONTROLLERS

CONTROLLER 1: AuthController
------------------------------
Handles login, logout, and registration for all users.

Actions / Methods:
- showLoginForm()      --> Opens the login page
- login()              --> Checks credentials and gives access
- logout()             --> Logs user out and clears session
- showRegisterForm()   --> Opens the customer registration form
- register()           --> Saves the new customer to database
- showChangePassword() --> Opens change password form
- changePassword()     --> Saves the new password


CONTROLLER 2: DashboardController
-----------------------------------
Shows the main homepage after login.

Actions / Methods:
- index()   --> Shows dashboard with summary cards:
                Admin sees: total active policies, total claims
                pending, revenue this month, upcoming renewals,
                lapsed policies count.
                Customer sees: their active policies, claim
                status, next payment due date.


CONTROLLER 3: CustomerController
----------------------------------
Handles all customer profile management (admin side).

Actions / Methods:
- index()    --> List all customers
- create()   --> Form to add a new customer
- store()    --> Save customer to database
- show()     --> View customer profile with their policies
- edit()     --> Edit customer information
- update()   --> Save changes
- destroy()  --> Delete customer (admin only)
- search()   --> Search by name, phone, customer ID


CONTROLLER 4: VehicleController
---------------------------------
Handles all vehicle information management.

Actions / Methods:
- index()    --> List all vehicles
- create()   --> Form to add a new vehicle
- store()    --> Save vehicle to database
- show()     --> View one vehicle's full details
- edit()     --> Edit vehicle information
- update()   --> Save changes
- destroy()  --> Delete vehicle (admin only)
- search()   --> Search by vehicle number, engine number,
                 body number, owner name


CONTROLLER 5: EstimateController
----------------------------------
Handles generating insurance quotes / estimates.

Actions / Methods:
- index()    --> List all estimates
- create()   --> Form to create a new estimate
- store()    --> Save estimate and calculate premium
- show()     --> View one estimate with full details
- edit()     --> Edit estimate
- update()   --> Save changes
- destroy()  --> Delete estimate
- print()    --> Generate printable estimate document


CONTROLLER 6: InsurancePolicyController
-----------------------------------------
Handles the full insurance policy lifecycle.

Actions / Methods:
- index()        --> List all policies (with status filter)
- create()       --> Form to create a new policy
- store()        --> Save the policy to database
- show()         --> View full policy details
- edit()         --> Edit policy information
- update()       --> Save changes
- destroy()      --> Cancel / delete policy (admin only)
- renew()        --> Renew an expiring policy
- generateDoc()  --> Generate downloadable policy document PDF
- lapsedList()   --> Show all lapsed policies
- renewalDue()   --> Show policies due for renewal


CONTROLLER 7: BillingController
---------------------------------
Handles payment and billing document management.

Actions / Methods:
- index()    --> List all billing records
- create()   --> Form to create a billing entry
- store()    --> Save billing record to database
- show()     --> View one billing document
- print()    --> Generate printable PDF bill
- markPaid() --> Mark a payment as completed


CONTROLLER 8: ClaimController
-------------------------------
Handles accident claims filed by customers.

Actions / Methods:
- index()    --> List all claims (with status filter)
- create()   --> Form for customer to file a claim
- store()    --> Save claim to database
- show()     --> View one claim with full details
- edit()     --> Edit claim details
- update()   --> Save changes
- approve()  --> Admin approves the claim
- reject()   --> Admin rejects the claim
- print()    --> Generate printable claim report


CONTROLLER 9: CompanyExpenseController
----------------------------------------
Handles recording and viewing company expenses.

Actions / Methods:
- index()    --> List all company expenses
- create()   --> Form to add a new expense
- store()    --> Save expense to database
- show()     --> View one expense record
- edit()     --> Edit expense
- update()   --> Save changes
- destroy()  --> Delete expense (admin only)


CONTROLLER 10: ReportController
---------------------------------
Handles generating and exporting all reports.

Actions / Methods:
- monthlySalesReport()   --> Monthly policy sales report
- vehicleWiseReport()    --> Analysis by vehicle type/model
- claimsReport()         --> All claims: pending, approved, rejected
- renewalsDueReport()    --> Policies expiring soon
- lapsedPoliciesReport() --> Policies that have expired without renewal
- exportPDF()            --> Export any report to PDF
- exportExcel()          --> Export any report to Excel

MODULES EXPLAINED ONE BY ONE


MODULE 1: LOGIN MODULE
-----------------------
Purpose: To allow only authorized users to access the system.

What happens:
- Admin logs in with admin username and password
- Customer logs in with their registered username and password
- If credentials are wrong, access is denied
- Each user sees only their own panel after login

Forms needed:
- Login Form (username + password)
- Registration Form (for new customers)
- Change Password Form


MODULE 2: CUSTOMER INFORMATION MODULE
---------------------------------------
Purpose: To store and manage all customer personal records.

What happens:
- Customer registers and their profile is saved
- Admin can view, edit, and manage all customer records
- Customer profile stores: name, address, phone, address proof
- All policies and claims are linked to the customer profile

Forms needed:
- Customer Registration Form
- Edit Customer Form

List pages:
- All Customers List with search bar


MODULE 3: VEHICLE INFORMATION MODULE
--------------------------------------
Purpose: To store complete details of each vehicle to be insured.

What happens:
- When a customer wants to buy insurance, their vehicle details
  are first recorded in the system
- Each vehicle has a unique combination of body number and
  engine number
- Vehicle rate (market value) is stored as it determines the
  insurance premium amount
- All policies and claims are linked to a specific vehicle

Forms needed:
- Add Vehicle Form
- Edit Vehicle Form

List pages:
- All Vehicles List
- Search by vehicle number, engine number, owner name


MODULE 4: ESTIMATE MODULE
---------------------------
Purpose: To generate an insurance quote for the customer before
they commit to buying a policy.

What happens:
- Customer or admin fills in vehicle details and selects a
  policy type (comprehensive, third-party, etc.)
- System calculates and displays the estimated premium
- Customer can review the estimate and then proceed to buy
- Estimate can be printed and shared with the customer

Estimate includes:
- Customer info
- Vehicle name, model, rate
- Vehicle warranty
- Policy type selected
- Estimated premium amount

Forms needed:
- Create Estimate Form

Print:
- Printable Estimate / Quote document


MODULE 5: INSURANCE PROCESS MODULE
-------------------------------------
Purpose: To formally create and manage the insurance policy
after the customer decides to buy.

What happens:
- Customer fills the full application form online
- System records all details: personal info, vehicle info,
  policy type, policy start date, policy duration
- A unique policy number is generated
- Payment is made and billing record is created
- Policy document is generated with all details
- Customer can download the policy document
- Policy status is tracked: active, expired, lapsed, cancelled

Forms needed:
- Insurance Application Form (full details)
- Policy Renewal Form

Documents generated:
- Policy Document PDF (downloadable by customer)


MODULE 6: COMPANY BILLING POLICY MODULE
-----------------------------------------
Purpose: To generate bills and record all payment transactions.

What happens:
- After policy is created, a billing document is generated
- Bill includes: customer details, policy number, vehicle info,
  amount, payment date, bill number
- Admin can view all bills and their payment status
- Customer can view their billing history
- Bills can be printed as PDF

Forms needed:
- Create Billing Record Form

Print:
- Printable PDF Bill / Invoice


MODULE 7: CLAIM DETAILS MODULE
--------------------------------
Purpose: To manage accident claims filed by customers.

What happens:
- Customer logs in and fills the claim form after an accident
- Claim form captures: place of accident, date of accident,
  insured amount, claimable amount
- The claim is linked to the customer's active policy
- Admin reviews the claim and checks if policy is valid
- Admin either approves or rejects the claim
- If approved, the claimable amount is processed
- Claim status is updated and customer can track it

Forms needed:
- Claim Filing Form (for customer)
- Claim Review Form (for admin to approve/reject)

List pages:
- All Claims with status filter (pending/approved/rejected)


MODULE 8: COMPANY EXPENSES MODULE
-----------------------------------
Purpose: To record all internal expenses of the insurance
company for financial management.

What happens:
- Admin records each company expense with: type, amount, date
- Examples of expenses: rent, salaries, utilities, marketing
- All expenses are stored with date for monthly tracking
- This data feeds into the financial reports

Forms needed:
- Add Expense Form
- Edit Expense Form

List pages:
- All Expenses List with date filter


MODULE 9: REPORTS MODULE
--------------------------
Purpose: Gives management a complete view of business
performance and helps in making decisions.

Reports available:

1. Monthly Sales of Policies Report
   - How many policies were sold each month
   - Total premium revenue collected
   - Which policy type was most popular
   - Month-wise comparison

2. Vehicle Wise Analysis Report
   - Which vehicle models/types are most insured
   - Premium amounts by vehicle category
   - Risk analysis by vehicle type

3. Claims Report
   - Total claims filed in a period
   - Claims approved vs rejected
   - Total claimable amount paid out
   - Claims pending approval

4. Policies Due for Renewal Report
   - List of all policies expiring in the next 30/60/90 days
   - Customer contact info for follow-up
   - Policy details and renewal amount

5. Lapsed Policies Report
   - Policies that have expired without renewal
   - Customer details for re-engagement
   - Date of lapse and original policy info

All reports can be:
- Viewed on screen
- Exported to PDF
- Exported to Excel
